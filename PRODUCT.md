# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, potential clients, and collaborators looking to view my work, assess my skills, and potentially hire me or work with me.

## Product Purpose

To showcase my portfolio of projects, demonstrate my technical and design capabilities, and serve as a marketing tool to attract professional opportunities.

## Brand Personality

Playful, interactive, creative, and memorable. It should feel engaging and show personality rather than just being a static resume.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS templates (boring whites/grays, standard corporate layouts). It should not feel like a standard boilerplate or a dry corporate site.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the interactions and design itself prove my capabilities, rather than just writing about them.
2. **Memorable Interaction**: Use playful elements and animations to leave a lasting impression on visitors.
3. **Content First, Play Second**: Ensure the playful elements don't obscure the actual work or make it hard for recruiters to find key information.
4. **Distinctive Identity**: Stand out from typical portfolio templates by embracing a unique, creative aesthetic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ensure high contrast for readability, support keyboard navigation for interactive elements, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` settings for users who are sensitive to animations.
